Our Journey

Behind the scenes of Obscura

From a rough idea to a working AI study companion, here's the real, unfiltered story of how we built this, mistakes, late nights, and all.

Milestone 1

The idea

It started with a simple frustration, past papers scattered everywhere and no easy way to get help at 11pm before an exam. We sketched out what became Obscura on a whiteboard.

1 / 1
Milestone 2

First wireframes

Before writing a line of code, we mapped out every screen, chat, past papers, planner, timer, to make sure the whole experience actually made sense together.

1 / 1
Milestone 3

Building NESH's brain

We built the backend that lets NESH search real past papers and answer in context, RAG search, a FastAPI backend, and a lot of trial and error getting the answers to feel genuinely helpful.

1 / 1
Milestone 4

Working sessions

Countless hours together figuring things out, one focused session at a time.

1 / 1
Milestone 5

Writing the code

Line by line, things came together, plenty of debugging, plenty of "wait, why isn't this working" moments along the way.

1 / 1
Milestone 6

Working together

Some of our best problem-solving happened side by side, laptops open, ideas flowing.

1 / 1
Milestone 7

3D printing the shell

We designed and 3D printed our own enclosure for the NESH robot, ears, speaker grille, and a cutout for the screen, all from scratch and iterated by hand.

1 / 1
Milestone 8

Getting the screen working

The first big win on hardware, NESH's display actually running and responding, the moment this stopped being just a website and started being a real device.

1 / 1
Milestone 9

Bringing it all together

Piecing together the website, the chat interface, the Pomodoro planner, and the sign-up flow into one cohesive product, this is where it started feeling real.

1 / 1
Milestone 10

Spreading the word

Getting Obscura in front of people meant more than just code, we filmed our own ad, behind the scenes and all, to actually tell people this exists.

1 / 1
Milestone 11

Small wins along the way

Every working feature felt like a reason to celebrate, this project has been as much about the people as the product.

1 / 1
Where we are now

Obscura today

Still building, still improving, and still just a group of students trying to make studying a little less stressful for everyone else. Thanks for following along.

1 / 1
